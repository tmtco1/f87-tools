F87 Lighting

Not connected

This browser has no WebHID, so it can't talk to the keyboard. Open this page in Chrome, Edge or Chromium on a desktop.

Plug the F87 in with its USB cable (not 2.4 GHz or Bluetooth), press Connect, and pick the entry that lists a vendor-defined interface. The page reads the keyboard's own settings first, then every change is written straight back.

Per-key colours

Click or drag to paint, right-click a key to pick its colour. Painting only changes this page; Upload & show sends it to the keyboard. Only profile 1 has been tested.
